## How to Run Locally:
In order to run this site locally on whatever device you are using (optimally a desktop computer), download and unzip the entire site folder/repo.
Then, open the file 'index.html' using your preferred browser, and voila, it should work without any further tweaking. 

## Project Overview:
This site is for a fictional fantasy business called 'Pixie Potions'. 
Pixie Potions sells remedial and recreational potions in a small fantasy community.
Whilst it is true that their potions work as well as advertised, the company is not forthcoming with the prices for their goods.
The only things that folks seem to know for certain are as follows:
1) Lady Alice is masterful at her work, and 
2) There are cautionary rumors surrounding the apothecaries' eagerness to make your acquaintance by full name.

## Pages List:
- **Home:** The Home page has a small 'About Us' Section for the business, and outlines what Pixie Potions sells as well as how to get to their storefront.
- **Catalogue:** The Catalogue page lists and describes the most popular potions sold by the business.
- **Testimonials:** This page has three testimonials by happy customers of the business in order to build credibility.
- **Contact Us:** The Contact page allows visitors to the website to make any comments, questions, or concerns that they may have directly to the business itself.
- **FAQ's:** This page outlines and answers the most frequently asked questions that Pixie Potions gets from the public and its customers.
- **404:** In addition to the standard front-facing pages for the site, there is also a custom 404 'page not found' page that includes a link back to the homepage.
